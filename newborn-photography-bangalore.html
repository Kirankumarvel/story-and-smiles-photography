What is a newborn photoshoot?

A newborn photoshoot documents your baby in the first two weeks of life, when they are still sleepy and curl naturally. The safest sessions are baby-led: the photographer follows the baby's cues, keeps the room warm, and never forces a pose. Most sessions happen at home so parents and siblings can be present.

FAQs

Safety and timing questions we hear weekly

When should we schedule a newborn session?

Schedule the session while you're still pregnant — around 32 to 36 weeks. We pencil in your due date and confirm once your baby arrives. The ideal shooting window is days 5 to 14, when your baby is still sleepy and curls naturally. If you go past 14 days, don't worry — we adjust to your baby's pace.

Is in-home safer than studio?

Both can be safe. In-home is our default for two reasons: the baby stays in familiar smells and sounds, and there's no car journey or strange environment in those first two weeks. Parents are also more relaxed. If you prefer studio, we can do that too — but in-home is what most parents choose, and what we recommend for the first 14 days.

What if my baby is unsettled during the session?

That's normal, and expected. We build in feeding and settling time, so a two-hour session often runs to two-and-a-half or three hours. If the baby needs to feed, be changed, or just be held, we wait. We never rush a newborn. The session is entirely baby-led.

Do you bring props and lighting?

Yes. We bring soft blankets, baskets, neutral wraps and lighting — all washed, sanitised and prepped before we arrive. You don't need to provide props. If you have a family blanket or heirloom you want in the photos, tell us and we'll weave it in.

Are you trained in newborn safety?

Yes. Newborn posing is a specialised skill, and safety comes first. We keep the room at 26–28°C, use a spotter for any wrapped poses, and never force the baby into a position. The safest approach — and the one we follow — is baby-led: your baby sets the pace, and we work around it.

Can parents and siblings be in the photos?

Yes. Parents and siblings are welcome in the photos, and 78% of our galleries include at least one parent portrait. We usually shoot the solo newborn portraits first, then bring the family in for the last 30 minutes. Older siblings have short attention spans, so we plan around that.

How long does a session take?

Classic in-home sessions run two to three hours, with feeding and settling breaks built in. The average across 150+ sessions is 2 hours 40 minutes. Fresh 48 sessions run about 90 minutes. Extended family sessions run about three hours.

When do we get the photos?

Within ten days, and often sooner. You get an online gallery link by email, plus print rights. Albums and prints can be added once you've picked favourites. If you need a few images urgently for a birth announcement, tell us at booking — we can usually prioritise those.
