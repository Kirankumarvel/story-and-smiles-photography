What is a pre-wedding shoot?

A pre-wedding shoot is a couple's photoshoot done before the wedding, usually one to three months ahead. In Bangalore, most couples shoot outdoors — Lalbagh, Cubbon Park or Nandi Hills — because the light and greenery work year-round. Sessions run two to six hours and produce 60 to 150 edited images depending on the package.

FAQs

Pre-wedding questions we answer weekly

Where do you shoot pre-wedding in Bangalore?

Our most-requested locations are Nandi Hills and Lalbagh, followed by Cubbon Park, Bangalore Palace, Elements Resort and Mysore Palace. For destination shoots we cover Coorg, Hampi, Mysore and Goa. We plan each session around the light — golden hour at Nandi Hills or Bangalore Palace is very different from midday at Lalbagh. Tell us the mood you want, and we'll match the location to it.

When should we book?

Eight weeks ahead is the sweet spot for most couples, and four to six weeks minimum. For peak weekends — especially October to February, when Bangalore's weather is best — book two to three months ahead. Destination shoots need more lead time because travel and stay need coordination.

How many outfits and locations?

Half-Day includes one location and one outfit change — enough for a focused, editorial session. Full-Day includes two to three locations and two outfit changes, which is what most couples choose. Destination bookings cover two days and let us plan multiple looks without the clock pressing.

Indoor or outdoor — which is better?

Outdoor is the default in Bangalore — the light and greenery work year-round, and couples tend to relax faster outside. But we always keep a studio or covered fallback ready in case the weather turns. If you book during monsoon (June to September), plan for indoor with an outdoor backup, not the other way around.

Do you help with styling?

Yes. Once your date is confirmed, we send a styling guide — colours that work with each location, fabrics that read well on camera, and a few reference frames. We don't provide the outfits, but we tell you what will photograph well. For destination shoots, we plan the wardrobe across both days so the looks don't repeat.

What if it rains?

Bangalore rain is unpredictable, so every outdoor booking includes a studio or covered fallback at no extra charge. If the forecast is clearly bad 48 hours out, we'll suggest switching locations or shifting the date. Monsoon bookings are planned indoors from the start, with outdoor as a bonus if the weather opens up.

How many photos do we get?

Half-Day delivers 60+ edited images. Full-Day delivers 100+ edited images. Destination delivers 150+ edited images across both days. The average across 60+ pre-wedding shoots since 2022 is 84 images per final gallery. All images are colour-graded and lightly retouched, with print rights included.

When do we get the photos?

Pre-wedding galleries go out within 10 to 14 days — faster than wedding edits, because there's less volume and no album to design. A same-week preview of 15–20 highlights is available on request if you need images for a wedding website or save-the-date.
