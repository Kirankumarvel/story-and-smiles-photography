What is a maternity photoshoot?

A maternity photoshoot documents your pregnancy, usually between 28 and 34 weeks, when the bump is clearly visible and most mothers are still comfortable moving. A good session runs two hours, focuses on calm portraits rather than posed glamour, and delivers images you'd be happy to display at home.

FAQs

Maternity questions we answer weekly

When should I book a maternity shoot?

Between 28 and 34 weeks is ideal — the bump is clearly visible and most mothers are still comfortable moving around. Book the session itself two to four weeks in advance; if you want a specific weekend or outdoor location, give us four to six weeks.

Studio or at-home — which is better?

Studio gives you controlled light, a seamless backdrop and a calm, distraction-free space. At-home is better if you have an older child, want to include the nursery, or simply prefer not to travel. Both run two hours and include two outfit changes.

What should I wear?

Something fitted enough to show the bump — a bodycon dress, a soft knit, or a saree draped to highlight the curve. Bring two outfits: one neutral and calm, one with colour or texture. Avoid busy prints. We send a full outfit guide once your date is confirmed.

How long does a session take?

Two hours, including outfit changes and short breaks. Most sessions run closer to 1 hour 55 minutes. We build in time to rest, adjust the setup, and try a second look without rushing you.

Can my partner or older child be in the photos?

Yes. Partner and sibling portraits are included in every package at no extra cost. We usually shoot the solo maternity portraits first, then bring in the family for the last 20 to 30 minutes.

Can you keep the photos private?

Yes. If you prefer your maternity photos stay out of our portfolio and social media, tell us at booking. We keep them private by default and only share with your written permission.

How many photos do we get?

40+ edited high-resolution images per session. You receive an online gallery for viewing and download, plus print rights. If you want specific frames retouched in detail, let us know — there's no extra charge.

When do we get the photos?

Within ten days, and often sooner — our average across 200+ maternity sessions is 8 days. You get an online gallery link by email. Albums and prints can be added after you've picked your favourites.
