What does an event photographer do?

An event photographer covers a function end to end — arrivals, speeches, rituals, guest candids and group portraits — then delivers a curated set of edited images within a week. In Bangalore, most corporate events book one to four hours and family ceremonies book a full morning or evening.

FAQs

Event questions we answer weekly

What kinds of events do you cover?

Corporate functions, conferences, product launches, birthdays, anniversaries, housewarmings, upanayana, shastipurthi, cradle ceremonies, naming ceremonies, puberty functions and family reunions. If it's an event with people and a moment worth remembering, we probably cover it. Tell us the type and date at booking and we'll confirm fit.

How fast is the turnaround?

Edited galleries go out within seven days; the average across 800+ events since 2019 is 6 days. A 48-hour preview of 20–30 highlights is available on request, and same-day previews are available on the Half-Day and Full-Day packages. Tell us at booking if you need images urgently for a press release or social post.

Do you cover corporate events?

Yes. We cover conferences, product launches, town halls, award nights and corporate offsites. We can work to a shot list from your marketing or comms team, and deliver images that are ready for LinkedIn, internal newsletters and press. Corporate events usually book one to four hours.

Can you shoot upanayana or cradle ceremonies?

Yes. We shoot upanayana, shastipurthi, cradle ceremonies, naming ceremonies and puberty functions regularly. We know the flow of the rituals, so you won't need to explain the timeline. For ceremonies we usually recommend the Half-Day package so the whole morning or evening is covered without rushing.

How many photos do we get?

Hourly delivers 60+ edited images. Half-Day delivers 200+ edited images. Full-Day delivers 400+ edited images. The average across all events is 240 images per final gallery. All images are colour-corrected and lightly retouched; specific frames can be requested for full retouching at no extra cost.

Do you cover multi-day events?

Yes. Multi-day events — a three-day conference, a week-long family reunion, a multi-day wedding-adjacent ceremony — are quoted as a custom bundle. We block the dates, assign the crew, and deliver all galleries together so nothing gets split across weeks.

Do you offer video?

Yes. We offer a short highlight reel as an add-on to any event package, and full-length video coverage for larger functions. If you want both photos and video, ask about the combo pricing at booking — it's usually cheaper than booking the two separately.

What is the starting price?

Hourly coverage starts at ₹5,000, Half-Day at ₹12,000 and Full-Day at ₹20,000. Final quotes depend on location, duration, crew size and add-ons like a same-day preview, hardbound album or framed prints. Travel beyond 30km is quoted separately.
