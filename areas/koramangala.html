Koramangala books to a brief and a deadline — we plan for both

Koramangala books differently to our other areas. Planners here work to a run sheet and a hard start time — offsites, product launches, engagement parties where the wedding itself is booked elsewhere.

  • Sony World Signal is the bottleneck. Straight-line from Banashankari is 13 km, but the 80 Feet Road–to–Forum stretch at 9am or 6pm can eat 15–20 minutes of that trip.
  • Corporate briefs reach us two to four weeks out, sometimes days. We take shot lists from comms teams, sign NDAs, and quote same-day previews without a second conversation.
  • Most corporate work happens around the 5th Block offices and the 80 Feet Road coworking spaces. Engagements cluster in 1st and 4th Block restaurants and hotel banquet floors near the Forum end.
  • At an 80 Feet Road office, the loading bay sits twenty metres from the conference room — but only before 8am. We work backward from that, not forward from the studio.

If the planner has already booked the venue, send the address and start time — we'll tell you what the room actually looks like at that hour.

FAQs

Koramangala questions

Do you cover corporate events and offsites in Koramangala?

Yes — offsites, conferences, product launches, town halls and award nights. We take a shot list from your comms team and deliver press-ready images, usually within seven days. Most corporate bookings run one to four hours.

Can you shoot headshots or brand content at our office?

Yes. Headshots for 8–12 people take about 90 minutes with a portable backdrop and one light. Team candids, workspace shots and product-in-hand frames can happen on the same visit.

How do you handle traffic for an evening event in Koramangala?

We arrive before the 6pm crawl starts, not during it. For a 7pm event, we plan to be at the venue by 5:45. Traffic past Sony World Signal is the only real risk, and we build the buffer into every evening booking.

Does Koramangala count as outside your travel radius?

No. Koramangala is inside our standard coverage, 13 km from the studio. We plan the drive into the schedule, not onto the invoice. Outstation work — Mysore, Coorg and beyond — is quoted separately.

Can you work with our event planner and venue coordinator?

Yes — that's how most Koramangala bookings run. Send the run sheet and the planner's contact a week before; we'll confirm load-in times, where we can stand, and what the brief expects.
