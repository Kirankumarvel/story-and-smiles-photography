We know Jayanagar's ceremony calendar, not just its pincode

We work out of Banashankari, on the Jayanagar side of South Bangalore — close enough that the studio has become the default meeting point for families here.

  • Morning muhurthams here don't wait for traffic, so every Jayanagar booking carries a built-in travel buffer of 20–30 minutes. We'd rather stand at the venue early than walk in during the procession.
  • We shoot at kalyan mantapas across 4th Block and 9th Block — the ones near 30th Cross and the community hall by Ragigudda Anjaneya Temple are the two we know best.
  • Most of what we're booked for here is family ceremony work — namakarana, seemantham, upanayana and temple weddings — rather than large banquet receptions you'd see further east.
  • First meetings usually happen at the 4th Block shopping complex or the Ragigudda side — easy to find, easy to park near, close to most residential blocks.
  • At the Ragigudda hall, parking fills up early — we load gear the evening before rather than carry cases through a muhurtham crowd.

Multi-generation families here tend to want the elders photographed first, before the hall fills up — so the opening twenty minutes of a Jayanagar booking go to family portraits, not candids.

FAQs

Jayanagar questions

Do you charge extra to travel to Jayanagar?

No. Jayanagar is inside our standard South Bangalore coverage. The studio is in Banashankari, about 4 km away, and we build the drive into the schedule rather than onto the invoice. Outstation work — Mysore, Coorg and beyond — is quoted separately.

Which Jayanagar venues have you shot at?

We shoot at kalyan mantapas and community halls across Jayanagar's 4th Block, 9th Block and South End Circle. If your hall isn't one we've listed, send the name — we'll tell you honestly whether we've worked there and what the light and access are like.

How early should we book for a Jayanagar wedding?

Three to four months ahead for November–February weddings, when muhurtham dates cluster. Maternity sessions book four to six weeks ahead. We take one wedding per day, so a popular muhurtham date can go early — check before you fix the hall if the date is flexible.

Do you cover temple weddings and naming ceremonies in Jayanagar?

Regularly. Namakarana, seemantham, upanayana and temple weddings are most of what we shoot here, and the ritual order differs by family. Tell us which ceremonies you're doing and who leads them — we'd rather follow your priest's sequence than impose a standard one.

Can we do a pre-wedding shoot in Jayanagar?

Yes. Lalbagh is a short drive from Jayanagar and works well for early-morning sessions before the gates get busy. If you'd rather stay local, the quieter residential streets around 9th Block and the older Jayanagar homes give a more personal set of frames.
