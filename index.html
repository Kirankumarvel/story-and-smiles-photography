About the company

Candid photography, as it actually happened - Your day, unposed and unscripted!

Story and Smiles is a small photography company based in Banashankari, Bangalore, founded by Karthik in 2019. We shoot weddings, pre-wedding sessions, maternity, newborn and family events across Bengaluru and, on request, across Karnataka and South India — from Mysore and Coorg to Goa and Chennai.

Most of our work is candid. That means we stay out of the way while the muhurtham, the nalangu and the reception unfold, catching the moments families actually remember — the grandmothers laughing, the cousins holding on, the quiet minute right before the kanyadaanam. We don't pose for the sake of posing, and we don't interrupt a ritual to get a shot. If you're looking for a wedding photographer in Banashankari or anywhere else in Bangalore who understands South Indian ceremonies without being told what comes next, that's us.

Common questions

Before you get in touch

Where are you based and do you travel across Bengaluru?
Our office is in Banashankari and we regularly travel across Bengaluru and nearby towns for weddings, maternity, newborn and family events. Travel beyond 30 km is quoted separately.
How far in advance should I book a photoshoot?
Book three to six months ahead for popular wedding dates. Smaller events, maternity and newborn shoots can often be scheduled four to six weeks ahead.
What are your starting prices?
Full-day wedding coverage starts at ₹45,000. Half-day is ₹25,000, pre-wedding shoots are ₹15,000, maternity and baby shower sessions are ₹12,000, and newborn sessions start at ₹10,000. Final quotes depend on location, duration and add-ons. Full package breakdowns are on the wedding and pre-wedding pages.
How long until we receive our photos?
Weddings are delivered in three to four weeks. Maternity, newborn and baby shoots usually come back sooner, often within ten days.
Do you offer videography as well?
Yes. We shoot cinematic wedding films alongside stills, and offer photo and video combo packages. Video details are on the event photography page.
Do you provide albums and prints?
Yes. We offer handcrafted albums (Karizma, Canvera and custom photo books), fine-art prints and canvas frames as add-ons to any package.
What kind of booking advance do you take?
A 25 to 30 percent advance confirms your date. The balance is split into two milestones, usually one before the event and one at final album delivery. We accept UPI, bank transfer and cash.
How do we book you?
Reach us on WhatsApp or call +91 98458 08890. You can also use the contact form below. We confirm your date once the booking advance is received.
