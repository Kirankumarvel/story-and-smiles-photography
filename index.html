FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about our wedding, maternity, newborn & event photography.

Are you based in Banashankari? Do you travel across Bengaluru?

Yes, we are based in Banashankari and happily travel across Bengaluru and nearby areas for weddings, maternity, newborn and family events. We bring the same candid, storytelling style to every location.

How far in advance should I book?

For popular wedding dates, we recommend booking at least 3–6 months in advance. Smaller events, maternity and baby shoots can often be scheduled 4–6 weeks ahead, depending on availability.

What wedding photography packages do you offer?

We offer custom packages for full-day and half-day wedding coverage, pre-wedding shoots, mehendi, sangeet and reception. Each includes candid photography, family portraits, and a curated set of high-resolution edited images.

When is the best time for a maternity shoot?

The ideal time is between 28–34 weeks of pregnancy, when the bump is clearly visible and you're still comfortable. For twins or medical considerations, we may suggest an earlier week.

How do you ensure safety during newborn shoots?

Newborn safety is our top priority. We keep the room warm, use soft props, follow baby-led posing, and never force positions. Sessions are relaxed with breaks for feeding and cuddles.

What are your starting prices?

Pricing depends on hours, location and services. We share customised quotes for each event. A booking advance confirms your date, with the remaining payment split across milestones.

How long until we receive our photos?

For weddings, we deliver curated, edited photos within a few weeks. Maternity, newborn and baby shoots are usually delivered sooner, so you can quickly use them for announcements and framing.

Do you offer videography as well?

Yes, we offer cinematic wedding videography alongside photography. Our team captures the essence of your day with a highlight film and full ceremony coverage. Ask us about our photo + video combo packages.

Can we choose the location for our pre-wedding shoot?

Absolutely! We work with you to select the perfect location — whether it's a scenic outdoor spot in Bangalore, a heritage site, or a place that holds special meaning for you both.

Do you provide albums and prints?

Yes, we offer premium handcrafted albums, prints, and canvas frames as add-ons. These make for beautiful keepsakes and gifts for family members.

What happens if it rains on the wedding day?

We have backup plans for indoor and covered outdoor spaces. We also carry professional lighting to adapt to any weather condition, ensuring your photos are stunning rain or shine.

Do you cover destination weddings?

Yes, we love destination weddings! We've covered events in Goa, Kerala, Tamil Nadu, and beyond. We handle all travel logistics so you can focus on your celebration.

How many photos do we get?

For a full-day wedding, we typically deliver 600–1000+ high-resolution edited images. Pre-wedding shoots usually yield 100–200 beautifully edited photos.

Can we request specific shots or poses?

Of course! We create a shot list with you before the event. We blend your must-have shots with our candid storytelling style for a perfect mix.

How do we book you?

Simply reach out via our contact form, WhatsApp, or phone. We'll discuss your requirements, share our packages, and confirm your date with a booking advance. It's that easy!
