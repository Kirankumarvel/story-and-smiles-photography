About Us

A photography Company in Banashankari, Bangalore that treats your day like family

Story and Smiles is a photography company in Banashankari, Bengaluru, founded in 2019 by Karthik. The team shoots weddings at popular venues like Kalyan Mantapas in Jayanagar and JP Nagar, banquet halls along Kanakapura Road, and resorts on the outskirts. They also cover pre-weddings, maternity, newborn and family events across South India.

Karthik left a desk job in 2019 to shoot weddings full time. What started with friends' weddings in Jayanagar has grown into a small team that covers events across Karnataka, Tamil Nadu, Telangana, Andhra Pradesh, Goa and Puducherry. They have travelled as far as Lucknow for a wedding.

The approach stays the same wherever the event is: stay close to the family, stay out of the way, and let the day unfold. Karthik still edits every wedding album himself.

FAQs

Questions we get before every booking

Are you based in Banashankari and do you travel across Bengaluru?

Yes. Our studio is in Banashankari and we regularly travel across Bengaluru and nearby towns for weddings, maternity, newborn and family events. Travel beyond 30 km is quoted separately.

How far in advance should I book a wedding photographer?

Book three to six months ahead for popular wedding dates. Smaller events, maternity and baby shoots can often be scheduled four to six weeks ahead.

What wedding photography packages do you offer?

We offer custom packages for full-day and half-day wedding coverage, plus pre-wedding shoots, mehendi, sangeet and reception. Every package is built around your timeline.

When is the best time for a maternity shoot?

Between 28 and 34 weeks of pregnancy. The bump is clearly visible and most mothers are still comfortable moving around.

How do you ensure safety during newborn shoots?

Newborn safety comes first. We keep the room warm, use soft props, follow baby-led posing and never force a position. Parents stay close throughout the session.

What are your starting prices?

Full-day wedding coverage starts at Rs 45,000. Half-day is Rs 25,000, pre-wedding shoots are Rs 15,000, maternity and baby shower sessions are Rs 12,000, and newborn sessions start at Rs 10,000. Final quotes depend on location, duration and add-ons.

How long until we receive our photos?

Weddings are delivered in three to four weeks. Maternity, newborn and baby shoots usually come back sooner, often within ten days.

Do you offer videography as well?

Yes. We shoot cinematic wedding films alongside stills. Ask about our photo and video combo packages.

Can we choose the location for our pre-wedding shoot?

Yes. We work with you to pick a location in or around Bangalore that fits the mood you want, and we can suggest options based on the season.

Do you provide albums and prints?

Yes. We offer handcrafted albums, fine-art prints and canvas frames as add-ons to any package.

What happens if it rains on the wedding day?

We plan for indoor and covered outdoor backups and carry professional lighting, so the day runs smoothly whatever the weather does.

Do you cover destination weddings?

Yes. We have covered weddings in Goa, Kerala and Tamil Nadu, and we travel across India on request.

How many photos do we get?

A full-day wedding usually delivers 600 to 1000+ edited high-resolution images, depending on the number of events and hours.

Can we request specific shots or poses?

Yes. We build a shot list with you before the event and blend your must-haves with the candid moments as they happen.

How do we book you?

Reach us on WhatsApp or call +91-98458 08890. You can also use the contact form on this site. We confirm your date once the booking advance is received.

How many photographers come to the wedding?

Half-day coverage comes with one photographer. Full-day packages include two, and we add a third for large weddings or when both sides need parallel coverage. Videographers are booked separately or bundled in a combo package.

Can you cover both the bride's and groom's side?

Yes. For full-day weddings we usually split into two teams in the morning, then merge for the muhurtham and reception. If you want this, let us know at booking so we can plan the crew and travel.

Do you understand South Indian wedding rituals and customs?

Yes. We shoot Kannada, Tamil, Telugu, Malayali and Konkani weddings regularly and know the flow of muhurtham, kanyadaanam, jeelakarra bellam, saptapadi, nalangu and reception. You will not need to explain the timeline to us.

What kind of booking advance do you take?

A 25 to 30 percent advance confirms your date. The balance is split into two milestones, usually one before the event and one at final album delivery. We accept UPI, bank transfer and cash.

Can we get a same-day preview for social media?

Yes, on request. We send a small set of edited highlights within 24 to 48 hours so you can post while the moment is still fresh. Full edits follow in the regular timeline.

Do you cover haldi, mehendi and sangeet separately?

Yes. Each of these can be added to a wedding package or booked as a standalone event. Most families combine mehendi and sangeet on one evening and keep haldi as a short morning shoot.

Are drone shots included?

Drone coverage is an add-on. It works well for outdoor weddings and destination events. We fly only where local rules allow and where the venue gives us clearance, so please confirm in advance.

Do you deliver RAW files?

We do not share RAW files as part of standard packages. We deliver fully edited high-resolution JPEGs, and RAW delivery can be discussed separately if you have a specific need.
