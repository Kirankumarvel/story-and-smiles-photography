FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Are you based in Banashankari? Do you travel across Bengaluru?

Yes, we are based in Banashankari and happily travel across Bengaluru and nearby areas for weddings, maternity, newborn and family events.

How far in advance should I book?

For popular wedding dates, we recommend booking at least 3–6 months in advance. Smaller events, maternity and baby shoots can often be scheduled 4–6 weeks ahead.

What wedding photography packages do you offer?

We offer custom packages for full-day and half-day wedding coverage, pre-wedding shoots, mehendi, sangeet and reception.

When is the best time for a maternity shoot?

The ideal time is between 28–34 weeks of pregnancy, when the bump is clearly visible and you're still comfortable.

How do you ensure safety during newborn shoots?

Newborn safety is our top priority. We keep the room warm, use soft props, follow baby-led posing, and never force positions.

What are your starting prices?

Full-day wedding coverage starts at ₹45,000, half-day at ₹25,000, pre-wedding shoots at ₹15,000, maternity and baby shower sessions at ₹12,000, and newborn sessions at ₹10,000. Final quotes depend on location, duration and add-ons.

How long until we receive our photos?

For weddings, we deliver curated, edited photos within 3–4 weeks. Maternity, newborn and baby shoots are usually delivered sooner.

Do you offer videography as well?

Yes, we offer cinematic wedding videography alongside photography. Ask us about our photo + video combo packages.

Can we choose the location for our pre-wedding shoot?

Absolutely! We work with you to select the perfect location in or around Bangalore.

Do you provide albums and prints?

Yes, we offer premium handcrafted albums, prints, and canvas frames as add-ons.

What happens if it rains on the wedding day?

We have backup plans for indoor and covered outdoor spaces, and carry professional lighting to adapt to any weather.

Do you cover destination weddings?

Yes, we love destination weddings! We've covered events in Goa, Kerala, Tamil Nadu, and beyond.

How many photos do we get?

For a full-day wedding, we typically deliver 600–1000+ high-resolution edited images.

Can we request specific shots or poses?

Of course! We create a shot list with you before the event and blend your must-have shots with our candid style.

How do we book you?

Simply reach out via our contact form, WhatsApp, or phone at +91-98458 08890. We'll confirm your date with a booking advance.
