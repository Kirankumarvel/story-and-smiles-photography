FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about our wedding, maternity, newborn and event photography in Bengaluru.

Are you based in Banashankari, and do you travel across Bengaluru for shoots?

Yes, Story and Smiles Photography is based in Banashankari, Bengaluru, and we happily travel across the city and nearby areas for weddings, maternity shoots, newborn sessions and family events. Whether your venue is in JP Nagar, Jayanagar, RR Nagar or elsewhere in Bengaluru, we bring the same candid, storytelling style to every location.

How far in advance should we book you for our wedding or event date?

For popular dates, we recommend booking your wedding photographer in Bengaluru at least 3–6 months in advance, especially for peak season weekends. Smaller events, maternity sessions and baby shoots in Banashankari can sometimes be scheduled 4–6 weeks ahead, depending on availability.

What wedding photography packages do you offer for Bengaluru weddings?

We offer custom wedding photography packages for Bengaluru weddings, with options for full‑day and half‑day coverage, pre‑wedding shoots, mehendi, sangeet and reception. Each package includes candid wedding photography, essential family portraits and a curated set of professionally edited high‑resolution images.

When is the best time to schedule a maternity photoshoot?

The best time for maternity photography in Banashankari and across Bengaluru is usually between 28–34 weeks of pregnancy, when your baby bump is clearly visible and you still feel reasonably comfortable. If you are expecting twins or have medical considerations, we may suggest an earlier week within that window.

How do you ensure safety and comfort during newborn and baby shoots?

Newborn safety is our top priority. We keep the room warm, use soft, clean props, follow baby‑led posing and never force uncomfortable positions. Sessions are slow and relaxed, with plenty of breaks for feeding and cuddles, so your newborn photography in Bengaluru feels safe and stress‑free for both baby and parents.

What are your starting prices and how does the booking process work?

Pricing depends on hours, locations and services (photo, video, albums), so we share customised quotes for each Bengaluru wedding, maternity or event. To confirm your booking, we collect a booking advance and share a simple agreement covering coverage, deliverables and timelines, with the remaining payment split across agreed milestones.

What is your usual delivery time for photos and films after the event?

For most weddings in Bengaluru, we deliver the curated, edited photos within a few weeks and wedding films a little later, depending on the package and season. For maternity, newborn and baby shoots, we usually share edited images sooner so you can quickly use them for announcements, sharing and framing.
