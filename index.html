About Us

A studio that feels like family.

Story and Smiles was started in 2019 out of a small studio in Banashankari. Seven years and 869 events later, the team is still the same handful of photographers who care about the small things — the grandmother's laugh, the baby's first yawn, the moment right before the muhurtham.

We don't pose you. We follow the day as it happens and step in only when you want a portrait. Most families tell us they forgot the camera was there.

Frequently asked questions.

The questions we get most, with short honest answers.

What wedding photography packages do you offer?

We offer half-day (₹25,000) and full-day (₹45,000) wedding packages, plus separate candid, traditional and cinematic coverage. See the wedding photography page for what each package covers.

When is the best time for a maternity shoot?

Most clients book between 28 and 34 weeks, when the belly is well-defined and you're still comfortable moving. The maternity page covers studio and at-home options.

How do you ensure safety during newborn shoots?

Sessions are baby-led with frequent feeds and cuddles, and every pose is supported by a trained assistant — never balanced unsupported. Full session details are on the newborn page.

Can we choose the location for our pre-wedding shoot?

Yes — outdoor, heritage or studio, within Bangalore or a short drive out. Location lists per area are on the pre-wedding page.

Do you cover destination weddings?

Yes. We travel across Karnataka, Tamil Nadu, Andhra Pradesh, Telangana, Goa and Puducherry. Mysore-specific logistics are on the Mysore page.

Do you offer videography as well?

Yes — cinematic and candid videography can be bundled with photography. Video is bundled into the event photography page.

How far in advance should we book?

For weddings, 4–8 months ahead is ideal, especially during muhurtham season. For maternity and newborn, 4–6 weeks is usually enough.

What is the delivery timeline?

A preview set within 7 days, the full edited gallery within 3–4 weeks, and printed albums within 6–8 weeks of final selection.
